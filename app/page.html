Where do I find this file?
  • AncestryDNA: in your account, open your DNA settings and choose to download your raw DNA data. Ancestry emails you a link to a .zip file.
  • 23andMe: in your account settings, choose to download your raw data.
  • Menu names change from time to time. If you cannot find it, search the company's help pages for "download raw data."

This program reads only the markers in its library and ignores the rest of your file. Nothing is uploaded.
